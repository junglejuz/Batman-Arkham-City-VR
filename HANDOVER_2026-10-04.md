## NEXT: grapple reticle (JJ will walk around manually and press F2 near a grapple point)

**Finding.** With the HUD drawn into the eye images (`bc hud off`), the grapple reticle sits at
the SAME pixel in both eyes while its gargoyle is about 200 px apart between them. The game places
world markers once per frame from its own camera, with no head and no per-eye offset. On the HUD
panel it therefore floats, and does not follow the target or its depth.

**Placement data.** Every Scaleform HUD draw has a 4×4 matrix in VS cb0:
- rows 0–1 are the scale (about 1e-4 to 1e-5: twips → NDC);
- row 3 is the translation `(tx, ty, 0, 1)` in NDC (y up).

Turning the game camera moves the compass ticks, the compass letters and the compass objective
icon (all at y ≈ 0.55). So "moves with the camera" alone can't separate world markers from
compass content.

**Probe now in the build** (`scene.cpp`, marker probe) — waiting on JJ's capture near a grapple point:
- hooks `ARPlayerController` vtbl+0x574 = `VectorToScreen` 0xD87D70 (thiscall: out FVector*,
  world FVector, void*; ret 0x14), which logs the world position plus the screen position;
- hooks `Canvas.Project` (0x8FDF60) and `Canvas.WorldToPixel` (0x681F80) exec natives, which log
  the screen result only;
- in a scene with no markers all of these logged **0 calls**. So check JJ's capture: if they are
  still 0 near a grapple point, the reticle is positioned some other way (look at the GFx movie's
  own script, or `ARGFxHUD` natives in `re/natives.txt`).

**Plan, following AKVR's final approach (memories `ak-reticle-band-and-number-depth`, `ak-markers-face-the-eye`):**
1. Get the world point behind each marker from the projection hook. That also gives the true depth.
2. Classify a HUD draw as a marker when its translation matches a projected point from this frame.
3. Keep markers OUT of the panel (in the eye images). Per draw and per eye, add
   `(eye-projected point − game-projected point)` to the translation in cb0, by uploading a
   patched copy before the draw. The detective-constant patching in `vision.cpp` shows how.
   The current eye on the render thread comes from the BeginDrawing label (`begin_exec_detour`).
4. Give markers a facing / scale correction like AKVR TARGETFACE if they look turned.
